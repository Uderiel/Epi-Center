# Charity wardrobe and household sharing — approved design

Existing approved anchors: neutral everyday clothing/comfortable flats, two real work outfits, practical sleepwear, actual currently worn outfit loan rather than magical clothing transformation. Approved review art shows an olive tee, cream cardigan, jeans and brown flats; the portrait alone is not a full inventory authorization.

## Approved two work outfits

- Normal outfit: olive simple T-shirt, cream cardigan, blue jeans, brown comfortable flats, plain neutral bra and underwear; no socks assumed with these flats.
- Clean spare: neutral gray simple T-shirt, oatmeal cardigan, blue jeans, a second pair of brown comfortable flats, plain neutral bra and underwear.

Every garment is a separate actual owned item. The first handover loans whichever complete outfit she is really wearing, including its actual condition, rather than spawning a pristine duplicate. She changes privately into practical sleepwear; after rest/shower she uses the other clean outfit. Garments do not grant unapproved attribute bonuses. Keys, money, jewelry and inventory are not automatically transferred by an outfit loan. Laundry still requires real items/time; no automatic cleaning on handover. Return of loaned garments follows the final release process.

Owner approves sleepwear as a plain cotton T-shirt and loose pajama trousers. Owner also approves one soft towel, one spare towel, one washcloth, hand soap, toothpaste and shampoo. These are design approvals, not implemented inventory. Exact reserve underwear/sock quantities and timed laundry remain unresolved.

## Minimalism and ordinary household sharing during the lock

Charity is a minimalist. She knows she cannot leave Cherished Touch and keeps a modest practical household rather than a large wardrobe, luxury collection or unexplained offsite lifestyle. Her confinement is an established private constraint, not permission for public AI dialogue to reveal hidden causes or lore.

During the three-day period both occupants have Charity’s appearance, and **they can share practically all ordinary household belongings**. Do not enforce a rigid Charity-only/player-only split for ordinary food, clothing, care supplies, towels, furniture and amenities. This includes the ordinary personal treat stock; actual contents remain finite. If the player prefers separate personal things, respect that preference where real available items permit it rather than forcing sharing. Separate toothbrushes remain available stock, not a universal story restriction that overrides this sharing direction.

Sharing is use of real tracked items, not cloned clothes, simultaneous possession of one garment, overwritten ownership or free inventory withdrawals. Keep original player belongings and restoration records intact. This is a household-sharing permission during the locked stay, not blanket public access, a monetary ownership transfer, equipping protected quest suits or disclosure of the hidden hatch. Real Charity and the player remain distinct people, not a merged mind.

## Approved modest cleaning

Light daily tidying and a short weekly clean fit her minimalist routine. Cleaning should fit quiet/off-duty time and never block the exit. Track actual garment/towel condition and laundering rather than resetting all items on render. No exact new chore duration, chemical effect, fee or surprise quest is established.

## Approved response to sharing objections

If the player complains about sharing, Charity initially teases and jokes lightheartedly that they are sharing practically everything already and asks what they have that she does not. She makes light of their matching bodies/situation rather than treating ordinary household items as sharply divided possessions. If the player persists, she relents and accommodates separation using real available items. No new refusal count, cost, relationship penalty or forced sharing is authorized. Her “same person” jokes describe the disguise, not actual merged consciousness or knowledge that both feel identical emotions; player discomfort is not overwritten by narration.
