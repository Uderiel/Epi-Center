Discoverable features:

- Cardinal areas 2, 4, 6, 8 have six park benches each.
- Shrubbery, bushes and floral arrangements throughout all eight areas.
- Equidistant lamp posts provide night illumination.
- Each area has a hidden Hub Cylinder slot which only appears when the matching numbered cylinder is brought to the correct location. Before its return, a searchable manhole-sized circular cover is visible, with an arrow pointing toward the Spire. Preserve the difference between the visible cover and the hidden/activated slot.
- Example #3 (NE): a search before the cylinder return finds a manhole-sized circle pointing SW. The sketch labels a parted manhole cover, clamps, glowing nodes/lines and a raised cylinder receptacle. After return the lid closes but remains raised from its normal position.

The slot sketch marks a three-foot rise and a handwritten diameter that appears to read 75.4 inches. Confirm the latter before dimensioned art or physical interactions depend on it.
