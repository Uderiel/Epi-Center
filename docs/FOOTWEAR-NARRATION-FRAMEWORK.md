# Private footwear narration — v1.0.101

Owner requested periodic, experiential narration of ordinary footwear sensations and explicitly excluded fungal infection. `footwear-senses.js` supplies selected, bounded observations from the live flats state and Priya’s actual activity. It does not introduce a second consciousness or give the item speech or independent movement.

The snapshot distinguishes worn, carried/park and closet states; alternating walking pressure, reduced seated desk contact and stationary contact; enclosed warmth and light perspiration after real worn time; ordinary worn-shoe odor and faint herbal perfume; rough ground-level sight and plainly local sound. No infection, disease, decay, medical diagnosis, remote conversation, mind-reading, invented dialogue or commanded player emotion is supplied. Desk-copy activities use the authored seated-work interpretation; there is no new posture/foot-pressure simulation.

A saved thirty-game-minute cadence prevents duplicate pulses after reload. A bulk time advance coalesces to one newest sensory observation while retaining all mechanical receipts in order. No rendering or offline clock creates observations or provider calls. Different focal text avoids listing every sensation on every pulse. The manual nearby-senses control can request a current observation without altering recovery cooldowns.

Authored fallback works with AI off or unavailable. Existing private backend, access code, AI-enabled and narration controls govern generation; Settings adds an independent periodic-footwear opt-out. No provider key enters a save. `narrator:priya-flats` is a private narrator, separate from Priya dialogue and generic form personalities. Its packet contains the sensory snapshot, not wallet, inventory, private NPC history or hidden world facts. Existing actual NPC dialogue retains its existing AI adapter. This feature does not fabricate ambient NPC-to-NPC dialogue.

AI may vary brief second-person description but cannot move Priya, create visits, change senses/capabilities, advance time, roll, adjust Item-binding/mastery, grant escape or discover the player. The authored observation remains in Game result details. Existing client limits, budget, no-retry fallback, save-revision and scene guards apply; footwear place/activity/new observation/reversion also invalidate late responses. At most one coalesced observation per canonical advance is offered for generation, not an unlimited catch-up queue.

Verification includes pure sensory/cadence/privacy tests, mocked asynchronous generation and stale-response tests, plus actual browser client/settings tests using an isolated mock. Paid Featherless prose quality, provider availability and real-device experience remain owner checks; no live paid request or new backend deployment is claimed.
