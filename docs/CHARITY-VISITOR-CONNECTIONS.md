# Charity meal gifts and witnessed reports — local implementation

`charity-visitors.js` connects the approved community meals and ingratitude rules to actual presence and owned food. Existing served items now support a validated custody transfer: the same ready serving changes holder, leaves the giver's inventory/prepared-serving slot and keeps its source/receipt/container history. No food, money, pantry refill or reward is issued by prose. Real Charity thanks donors automatically, keeps real donated meals and consumes them through the shared serving system instead of spending backup ingredients. The player covering Charity can explicitly thank a giver or respond rudely. Those buttons do not alter player speech behind their back.

Only a present giver records the witnessed response. Reports wait for that same witness to meet actual Charity on her own shift; an absent giver cannot report telepathically. Charity apologizes to the giver and later confronts the player only in an actually shared private room. Stable gift IDs/replayed handovers do not create copies. Gift custody, conduct and incidents survive reloads. Current coverage/real-time block references identify the offending shift, with half-pay/no+5qualification recorded. **Actual wage deduction/reward correction is not implemented** because the owner-deferred coverage quest payroll ledger does not exist yet; no unrelated funds/relationship are changed. Do not claim the recorded incident is a paid wage adjustment.

Charity's provider context receives only actual received gifts and reported complaints, filtered to their applicable apparent identity, not unreported substitute encounters. Other NPCs receive no Charity visitor ledger. No shopper/meal-giver NPC roster, visit times, donor food creation, offline multiplayer service or hidden hatch qualification is invented by this adapter. Those real sources must supply their own presence/held-food events.

Six focused cases cover gift transfer/replay, consuming donated food before pantry ingredients, genuine donor-held meals, gratitude without rewards, witnessed rudeness, delayed/private complaints and reload ownership. Five actual browser-control checks verify donation, explicit rude choice, incident/serving persistence, unchanged other save and no uncaught errors using isolated synthetic fixtures. All96frontend suites pass in the broader story/visitor run; final affected tests were rerun after provider/privacy/appearance checks. Backend55cases pass. No owner save or paid model request used; unpublished.
