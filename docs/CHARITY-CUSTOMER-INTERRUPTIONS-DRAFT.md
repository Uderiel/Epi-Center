# Charity customer interactions and interruptions — consolidated review proposal

## Already approved; not being reopened

Gifted Gabber service; authoritative register prices/payment; privacy-respecting fitting-room checks; meals received from real visitors and appreciative service; initial six-to-seven-hour coverage; real-world six-hour locked-period rotation; safe release after72hours; no blanket player identity/relationship transfer. Short lone-staff breaks pause entry/checkout while existing shoppers browse/exit; recognized relationship−50or worse visitors are asked to leave first and she waits for actual departure. Quest, customer queue, offline simulation and real multiplayer occupancy are not implemented.

## Proposed remaining interaction rules

| Situation | Proposed behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Another shopper arrives during a conversation | Briefly acknowledge them, then finish the current transaction. Pause optional personal chatter and offer the waiting shopper help next. Arrival alone does not erase the ongoing speaker’s latest question. |
| Several shoppers need service | Maintain a simple first-come counter queue; browsing and optional conversation do not reserve every service indefinitely. Each basket/order/receipt belongs to the actual customer. No invented impatient exit timer, automatic sale, premium priority or relationship penalty. |
| Customer wants quiet or declines help | Respect the preference, remain available, and skip repeated offers/personal questions. Courtesy is not a requirement to force cheerful replies from another player. |
| A fitting room is requested | Check recorded occupancy/privacy, offer an actually free stall, or explain all are busy. Closed curtains alone do not prove an empty stall. Do not enter/open a curtain to check, evict an occupant or move another player without an authored action. |
| Staff needs a short bathroom/care break | Finish a currently committing payment, announce the pause, and use the approved lone-staff break policy when no alternate worker is actually available. Preserve baskets and allow existing shoppers to browse/leave. Urgent needs are not a reason to silently finish every waiting order first. |
| Normal staffing handover occurs mid-service | Carry the same basket/queue/order state to the actual replacement; do not restart checkout, duplicate goods/payment, delay the fixed rotation indefinitely or extend the hard seven-hour cap. A receipt already committed stays committed. |
| Locked staff player disconnects | Continue the already-approved offline service/rotation, with actual recorded work and once-only pay. Do not hand that player’s identity or rewards to another account or create a second copy of them. |
| A customer disconnects while browsing/waiting | Preserve their saved basket and release their active counter-queue place; reconnecting requires actual resumed service rather than an automatic sale or item confiscation. No new inactivity timer is specified. |
| Real danger or another situation needs Charity | Interrupt optional chat; alert the actual Charity where possible and follow existing danger/action systems. No teleportation, extra combat mechanic, new rescue guarantee or arbitrary early mask escape is added. Routine queues alone should not keep waking her from rest. |

## Limits

These are draft rules for owner review, not new implemented controls or penalties. Third-party speech, intentions, private knowledge, stock or consent are never authored solely to make the service flow succeed. Keep the already approved half-pay/no-relationship-reward consequence specific to rudeness toward helping meal-givers until broader misconduct consequences are separately approved. No new numerical action duration, patience threshold, payment fee or arbitrary score is proposed here.
