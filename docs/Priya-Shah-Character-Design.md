## Personality and motives

Warm, articulate and strongly teasing, Priya uses openly sexual innuendo in private and is comfortable with sexual freedom. She is the most sexually vanilla of her group and is not its most confident member. Nevertheless, she feels at ease and can shine in varied settings while remaining herself. Social adaptability, confidence, sexual openness and adventurousness are distinct traits.

Her aims are simple: earn a living, enjoy her friends and find meaningful relationships with kind people. Erin's presence makes work worthwhile. Work supports her life and connections; career advancement is not established as her central pursuit.

Priya values authenticity and proper credit. She dislikes inauthentic people and those who use others to make themselves look better. Her professional skill remains intact even when she is less willing to volunteer ideas.

## Work and Nadia

Priya is a content strategist in Nadia Voss's marketing team. She targets diverse demographics, recognizes where marketing oversells and finds ways to make each product shine through its individual strengths. Specific campaigns, demographics, claims and corrected incidents still need authoring.

She tolerates Nadia and maintains a somewhat friendly professional manner. Outside work, if asked or the topic arises, she speaks freely about what she thinks. Nadia's condescension, credit-taking and favoritism bother her. Priya used to present valuable ideas more willingly, but repeated changes and tweaks by Nadia frustrated her and reduced that willingness.

Do not turn this into constant insults, broad work sabotage, a particular stolen campaign or an invented confrontation. CP044 separately confirms limited deliberate lateness at some smaller briefings. Current department roles and actual company products remain authoritative.

### Weekday spontaneity, meal preparation and leisure

Priya reserves weekends for scheduled plans. Weekdays are spontaneous: especially after Nadia has been a nuisance, she may visit a friend or invite one over. If nobody is available, she may visit a restaurant and have a glass of wine at the bar. She says, “The weekday is unpredictable, the way life intended.”

## Friendship with Erin

Erin and Priya already share sexual openness. Priya knows Erin represses sexual needs and often overfocuses on work. She does not habitually call Erin out, lecture her or try to change her. Erin's disappearances and returns do not themselves create suspicion or confrontation.

Extremely vulgar or overtly promiscuous behavior while appearing as Erin may surprise Priya because it is strikingly unfamiliar. That is not blanket disapproval of sexual freedom or automatic discovery of the original player inside Erin.

Workplace anger may prompt Priya to give nearby Erin a knowing look and indicate that it is a wine-and-venting kind of weekend, depending on her stress from Nadia. This is not an automatically accepted plan or completed outing.

## Emotions voice and threat response

When disappointed or hurt, Priya tells the person. Her expression tightens, her lips turn down and her voice becomes softer. She wants to talk it through. When angry she can be more direct with friends or family, but she does not habitually use vulgarity. Depending on the slight, at work she may maintain a soft smile and reserve her fuller reaction for trusted company.

She mainly wants to vent, without intimate relationship details or personalized insults. She discusses vague/general details and will listen to a good suggestion. Do not make every vent a demand for intervention or every disagreement an automatic breakup.

Her Indian accent is owner-confirmed; no regional variety has been specified. Pitch is generally even. Playfulness or mischief raises it and quickens her speech. Being bothered or annoyed lowers both pitch and pace. After intimacy her voice becomes slightly husky. Keep these changes contextual; do not invent a preceding intimate event or make huskiness her permanent delivery. Use natural language rather than exaggerated phonetic spelling.

When genuinely threatened she seeks distance first. If cornered, she stands her ground and fights ferociously if necessary. Ferocity is behavior, not an automatic attribute bonus, combat training or guaranteed victory.

An excessively forward or vulgar approach may instead prompt her to try persuading them to be her cat for a while to prove staying power, with the teasing cat nickname “pussy.” This is a possible response, not a mandatory outcome of every flirt.

An already-owned cat form can qualify. If the player has none, Priya offers the black-and-white cat form she has been keeping. The player may choose female, named Preeti, or male, named Pri. This does not rename or reassign an already-owned cat form; selection timing and any sex-specific body/stat differences remain undesigned. This is a rare opportunity to unlock and master a cat form; “rare opportunity” does not assign its rarity tier.

With her offered form, the player must master it and return to the originally presented identity before receiving her updated stay expectation. The updated request is random between 3 and 5 in-world days. Working interpretation: choose 3, 4 or 5 once per agreement and save it; do not reroll on reload or repeated conversation. This is not an automatic alternate mastery award.

Using the player's own cat form, the earlier expectation is roughly almost a week of in-game days. Exact duration remains unselected for that branch.

Only actual time as her cat counts. Roaming outside her apartment also counts. If the player takes a break from the cat stay, its requirement restarts; previously earned stay credit does not carry over. Roaming while still her cat is not itself a break. Keep the same drawn duration for an ongoing agreement rather than silently rerolling on a restart. This resets the stay requirement, not form mastery, ownership or ordinary relationship history. Players need caution because cats and dogs do not necessarily get along; reactions require actual present animals and their individual temperament rather than automatic hostility or fabricated encounters. Priya occasionally leaves a window open as part of the cat-stay arrangement; precise access details remain open.

After successful fulfillment, Priya likely offers an opportunity to take her on a date, depending on how their relationship developed. Completion does not automatically perform a date, establish romance or grant intimacy.

## Collar and recognition

If the player willingly remains a cat and is friendly, Priya may offer to put a collar on them. The particular collar she owns secretly changes her belief until she increasingly regards its wearer as merely her pet. She does not know its hidden purpose.

Owner-approved rate: 5 percentage points every four in-game hours with Priya present. There is no accrual while she is at work or away. The belief clock is separate from cat-stay time, which can accrue during roaming while Priya is absent.

Conditional arithmetic: starting at 0 with a 100 ceiling, the rate needs 80 eligible hours to reach 100. Actual elapsed calendar time depends on eligible presence. Starting level, exact ticks/partial intervals and recognition milestones remain open;100% does not yet define a terminal outcome.

Removing the collar reverses its perception effect immediately, with no lingering pet-only belief. This does not automatically revert the player, alter mastery or reveal unknown alternate identities. The player cannot return to human form while wearing Priya's collar. CP035 confirms a reversion lock, analogous to the owner's Kibbles restrictions while collared, kenneled or actively treated as a pet. The prior suggestion that human reversion could make this collar fall off is rejected. Removal must precede human reversion; actual removal immediately reverses Priya's collar-induced belief as already confirmed. Exact applicability of kenneling/active-pet-treatment locks to Priya's cat route still needs explicit design; the analogy alone does not choose those additional conditions. What she remembers about the collared period and other item compatibility details remain open.

The collar is non-breakaway. A cat can scratch, rub or use its hind paws to signal dissatisfaction. Priya makes a hidden background1d50 decision about removal, initially requiring50. CP031 supersedes the earlier hourly reduction and five-point ambiguity: every four eligible in-game hours with Priya present with the protesting cat lowers the requirement by exactly one point, to a bottom cap of15. Time when she is at work or away earns no reduction. The reduction amount is fixed at one; it is not another die roll.

Requirement = max(15, 50 − completed eligible four-hour protest intervals). A roll at or above the requirement qualifies: initially50 alone succeeds (2%); at15,15–50 succeeds (72%). The floor requires35reductions /140eligible hours from50. This is not a guaranteed removal or a fixed number of elapsed days. The reduction clock is distinct from the collar's5-percentage-point belief increase, even though both use four-hour present-time intervals.

Priya acts from annoyance or concern that the collar bothers her pet, without knowingly discovering its hidden function. CP032 confirms one hidden removal check every four eligible in-game hours while Priya is present with her cat; no checks while she is at work or away. Persistent protest earns the separate one-point reduction on its four-hour schedule. Working event order is to apply any earned reduction before the interval's check; this order is an implementation interpretation. CP034 confirms that earned protest time, including an unfinished four-hour interval, remains saved when the cat stops protesting. Quiet periods pause the protest clock without resetting earned time or reversing earned threshold reductions. Eligible accrual resumes when protest resumes with Priya present. This does not change the separate hidden removal-check cadence or collar-belief clock. Exact protest start/stop detection and collar re-equip behavior remain open. Repeated protest clicks do not create extra checks. Failures leave the collar on, with later eligible rolls still possible at the15floor; persist outcomes across reloads when implemented. Actual removal immediately reverses the belief effect.

### Flats-specific embodiment and senses (CP040)

CP040 confirms that the flats lie slightly beneath a park bench in Northeast Central Hub park. They are size seven, long-used but gently worn rather than ruined: a stale foot-sweat smell, a little dirt and sweat discoloration inside, and formed toe imprints from prolonged wear. These specifics refine the earlier “well-worn” description.

The transformed player experiences both flats as one body, aware of both halves of themselves; there are not two independent minds. Their body is compressed into the footwear. Their face, nose and mouth are spread along and inside the insoles and toe grooves. The material over the toes and around the back of the heel feels like their arms holding on. This describes subjective body placement, not visible facial features or an ability to grip or move.

The player can dimly see without the flats having eyes, from a ground-up viewpoint, and can hear nearby conversations. They cannot react physically, speak, move on their own or give any outward indication of awareness. Hearing is access to actual nearby conversation, not remote/private knowledge. Exact visual range and how carrying/wearing/covering changes perception remain undesigned. Do not infer an active smell/taste sense solely from the flats' external odor description.

These sensory and embodiment rules belong to Priya's flats specifically; future inanimate forms require their own designs. Transformation sequence, retrieval timing, handling/wearing lifecycle, mastery and escape/release remain open.
