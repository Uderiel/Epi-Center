## Home life and guests

- Actual room use: bathroom for washing, closet for private outfit changes, kitchen for food, living room for visitors/media, bedroom for sleep.
- Warm meals, tea, snacks, dishes, light tidying and occasional laundry use real actions and stock. No full-house cleaning every day or automatic restocking.
- Coordinate existing wardrobe presets with work, home, hosting, yoga, jogging and bed. Retain individual item browsing, ownership and the unique flats lifecycle.
- Recognized friends may knock and visit when she is home and available. Priya may offer tea, a snack or a shared film; acceptance, preparation, presence and actual watching are separate recorded actions.
- An active conversation/accepted visit receives a natural wrap-up before a flexible routine resumes. Urgent work, a booked appointment or bedtime can end a visit, with an explicit farewell and physical departure rather than disappearance.
- Agreed outings/escorts include actual departure, travel, arrival and return. Visitors do not authorize intimacy or access to private belongings.
- Missing ingredients/products lead to an honest alternative or a saved shopping need. Errands do not purchase from an unimplemented shop or conjure goods.
