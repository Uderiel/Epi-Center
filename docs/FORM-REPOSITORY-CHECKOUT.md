# Repository checkout — local implementation, unpublished

The owner confirms Reception uses the established store purchase flow; opening stock is still a separate unfinished design. The existing Cherished Touch and Burke's stores each have their own checkout controller. Repository's small facility adapter follows that same review/pay/receipt convention and reuses RepositoryPricing, RepositoryArcade, FormStorage, Relationships, body capability checks, sleeping/conduct guards and the shared text-action bridge. It does not replace either existing store.

`repository-retail.js` starts with an empty stock ledger. Its trusted `receive` entry point accepts an actual authored shop delivery with a unique identity, complete unmastered profile and finite quantity. Replaying a delivery cannot add copies. No delivery runs in production yet. The same authoritative records feed physical display stands and Reception. Receiving QA stock is test setup, not approval of an opening quantity or restocking schedule. Consignment custody, settlement and trading remain separate future authoring; no player-owned form is silently taken or reset.

At Reception C an available manager reviews one actual offering, current courtesy discount and an optional actual arcade voucher. Reading/reviewing does not charge, consume a voucher, acquire a form or reserve stock. Payment rechecks identity, attendant, current price/eligibility, exact stock revision, funds and existing ownership. It stages native acquisition and voucher redemption before committing stock, gold, till, receipt and custody together. Repeated or stale payment cannot spend again. An existing form record is never overwritten or duplicated. The usual three carried slots and acquisition transport queue apply; purchase never auto-equips or masters the form. The customer's previous body resources are preserved.

Buttons and asserted typed requests use the same controller: “buy [listed name]” reviews; “pay” completes the pending purchase. Negation/private intentions do not constitute payment. Ambiguous listing names require choosing the actual listing. Eve's contextual guide explains the same flow. Services, trading and consignment have no invented prices or implementation from this checkout work.

Verification: nine native retail tests and isolated phone-width browser evidence in verification/repository-checkout-calendar-summary.json. Browser testing supplies synthetic stock through the actual receive controller, with AI disabled. No owner save, paid call, commit or publication. The live authoring catalogue remains empty until actual stock is designed and supplied.
