# Internal awareness and escape — player supplement

If Kibbles wins, full encasement can continue from Play. You remain aware inside a canine body. Kibbles communicates through scent, feeling, imagery, posture and sounds, not human speech.

Open **Form** or **Equip** to see influence, permanent mastery progress and the next internal opportunity. Normal play must advance 30 game minutes between internal actions; opening menus, reloading or waiting offline does not count. One-hour Wait is available outside combat. Opportunities do not stockpile.

Choose **Resist Influence** for 3 Resolve, **Desperate Resistance** for zero Resolve with a −2 roll penalty, or **Regain Composure** to recover 6 Resolve up to your current maximum. Resistance uses your original Willpower against the form's Reasoning. Success removes 10 influence and adds 10 durable progress; failure adds 5 influence but never erases earned progress. Legitimate resistance is not mistreatment. These actions themselves do not spend extra game time.

At zero influence and 100 progress, Common/Uncommon mastery becomes permanent automatically. Rare/Epic forms require one final opposed contest; failed finals retain progress. Legendary forms have their own trials. Erin's slow considerate cooperative route remains an independent alternative.

An eligible **regurgitation escape** can be attempted once per game hour, separately from internal resistance. It uses original Fitness and a d50 against rarity/influence difficulty. Kibbles has no zipper or seam. Escape releases you without granting mastery or healing. During a bored, unrestrained opening, an appealing play promise can instead support an original-Influence release attempt through that same hourly opportunity. Follow through on a promise while the dog remains nearby.

Respecting boundaries, care and keeping promises build meaningful relationship memories. Praise belongs after a helpful action. Food, bladder needs, play and real distractions influence the dog; trust is separate from control pressure. A new treat-reinforced cue takes about a game day, and repeated scent-marked routes about two. Canine paws cannot handle inventory treats; use food already accessible at the crate or an actual capable provider.

After mastery, Kibbles can offer sensory assistance and impulses, but your choices control your actions. You can release the form by will, then report Hangry Fangs to Eve in an appearance she can understand. Permanent name/gender choices and their matching portraits are available once after mastery. Mastery itself does not refill resources or award Tier Mastery XP.
