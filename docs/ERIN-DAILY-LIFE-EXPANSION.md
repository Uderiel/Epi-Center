## Workday behavior and useful office interactions

Standard work window: weekdays 09:00–17:00, matching Erin's current schedule. Individual arrivals between 08:40 and 09:00, two staggered lunch groups between 12:00 and 13:00, short afternoon breaks and departures after 17:00 provide variation. Exceptions must be stored commitments, not a random re-roll every minute. Do not punish Erin for a coworker's schedule change.

Leads begin in their own cubicle areas. A sales huddle and a marketing review use the existing Conference Room at distinct recorded times. Room reservations must reject overlaps and use real routes. Coworkers walk through actual office doors for meetings, equipment, lunch and departure; a schedule label alone does not make them present. An in-progress conversation or encounter holds the actor rather than teleporting them away.

The room's top level shows a compact People here list and a Workstations disclosure. A selected person's card shows Introduce/Talk, their current activity, and relevant work. Selecting a desk opens its computer, phone, drawers and personal effects beneath that desk. Inspecting personal effects does not put them in inventory. Give colleagues several authored replies tied to actual recent events before adding paid AI variety.

Initial complete task loops:

1. Daniel's briefing: accept at Sales Cubicles → collect a named approved update → book the available Conference Room → tell Daniel in person. Reserve actual time and preserve the pending task across reload.
2. Nadia's revision: accept at Marketing Cubicles → obtain Daniel's correction → give the marked version to Nadia. No completion by merely saying "done."
3. Staff introductions: Richard/Erin can introduce Michael to both leads and a small subset of coworkers, with memory of whom he has actually met.
4. Existing fax/copy/beverage duties remain available. New names attach to actual requests and document ownership; they do not duplicate the existing reward ledgers.

Ordinary paid-day work remains part of Erin's 350-gold workday. Richard's qualifying extra errands retain the separate once-only 50-gold bonus. His dialogue promises extra pay without stating the number. A team leader must not silently mint an extra bonus for every small favor.
