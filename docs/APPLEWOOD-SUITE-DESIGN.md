# Applewood Apartments — suites and regular units

Owner-confirmed architectural distinction, recorded during Richard Carter's Suite 7A design review. This is a design contract; it does not claim that new rooms, balcony traversal or storage capacity are implemented.

## Suite standard

Suites have larger rooms and more overall floor area than regular apartment units, providing additional furnishing/display space, usable floor space and storage. Richard's home gym fits this larger footprint; it is not an extra room required in every suite. Furnishings and lifestyle remain resident-specific.

Every suite includes an additional guest bedroom, a guest bathroom, a bathroom attached to the master bedroom, and a balcony with improved views over the city. The guest bathroom is closest to the kitchen. The guest bedroom is positioned between the entry hall and master bedroom. That positional relationship does not mean residents must walk through the guest bedroom to reach the master; final door adjacency must be explicitly authored. The master bathroom is accessed from the master bedroom. Balcony seating and looking out over the city should be real available activities when implemented; a better view does not reveal private occupants or grant travel shortcuts.

Regular units retain their established smaller footprints; this decision does not silently add guest bedrooms, second bathrooms or balconies to them. Suite classification must be explicit rather than inferred from a wealthy resident, decor or unit letter. Richard's 7A is confirmed as a suite; do not automatically reclassify Nadia's unit or other homes.

## Implementation requirements

Author a distinct expanded room graph and actual fixtures/storage for each suite; reuse shared residence navigation, drawers and permissions. Keep guest-accessible areas separate from private master-bedroom/bathroom access. Larger floor space permits more furnishings but is not an invented numerical inventory bonus. Preserve existing saves and room IDs or provide additive migration when an existing layout is expanded. Balcony access must not teleport or force risky movement. Current live build remains v1.0.107; suite implementation is pending.
