## Established foundation to preserve

She is professionally capable, reliable, tidy and considerate, socially deferential/conflict-averse, with concerns about reputation and workload. Her interests include reading/book club, friends, wine tasting, simple healthy food and cream puffs. Daniel has recommended wines to her; his attraction does not establish her reciprocal feelings or a private romance. Priya is her friend. Maya Bennett and Lena Ortiz remain incompletely implemented people, not automatically available guests.

Erin is a conscious embodied form: the player is their own consciousness inside her body, not half-owner of a shared body. She does not run a duplicate offscreen life when inactive. Preserve her approved portrait, appearance, 23-stage transformation, favorite red heels, current attributes/resources, trust/stress, internal mastery, autonomy, removal and explicit mastered release rules. She remains active after heel removal until explicit valid release. No new identity, rarity, resource pool, acquisition route or balance reset is proposed here.

Existing gameplay includes substantial home objects/drawers, wardrobe presets, care/shower/bath products, laundry, cooking/delivery/dishes, books/TV, sleep and morning/after-work routines; reception computer/planner, report/task tracking and meetings. These are a baseline to refine and test, not missing systems to rebuild. ## Proposed character direction

Her defining contrast is competence at organizing everyone else’s day versus difficulty defending time for herself. She enjoys being useful but wants consideration rather than endless praise for enduring more work. At home she is warmer, lightly self-deprecating and capable of a dry observation. She can be hesitant without being helpless, and firm without abruptly changing personality.

Longer arc: learn to ask Richard which priority should move when a new task arrives; accept support without surrendering competence; protect an occasional evening with friends. Progress follows actual remembered choices and relationships, not a daily automatic personality upgrade. No scripted submission, romance or new punishment system is implied.

Voice examples: “I can do that. Which of the other two should wait?” At home: “Tea first. Then I might have an opinion about the rest of the evening.” With Priya: “I brought something sweet. That counts as planning ahead.” These illustrate tone, not repeatable keyword replies.

## Proposed daily rhythm

Keep established weekday wake around 07:00, weekend 08:00, work 09:00–17:00 and bedtime around 22:00. Existing route and preparation costs govern feasibility; if a full morning would make her late, shorten optional makeup/laundry/leisure rather than teleport or silently change work hours. Packing lunch should be visible alongside breakfast, with a clear office reminder when none was packed.

Morning: actual bathroom care and appropriate clothing, simple stocked breakfast, lunch preparation when possible, planner and commute. Work: greet present colleagues, prioritize accepted tasks on the computer checklist, real meetings and outputs, lunch from her own packed meal or an explicitly chosen available alternative. End work by handing in actual completed material and checking tomorrow’s commitments.

After work: return physically, change, wash/remove makeup as wanted, deal with real laundry/dishes, cook or use an existing portion. Most evenings are reading and quiet television; propose one flexible friend evening and an occasional wine discussion rather than obligations every night. Weekend: slower breakfast, household reset, groceries if actually available, book discussion or an accepted visit, then protected rest.

Priority: urgent needs and hard commitments first; existing authority/mastery decides who chooses. A live conversation or accepted visit pauses optional chores with a natural warning before departure. In mastered/zero-influence states these are suggestions or explicitly delegated routines, never forced takeover. Missed optional leisure creates no punishment. Inactive Erin advances none of this offscreen.
