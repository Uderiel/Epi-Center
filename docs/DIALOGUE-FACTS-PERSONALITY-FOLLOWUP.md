## Changes made

- **Concrete wishes:** Tits initially understands the approved example as wanting to be a sexy bimbo like her. A read-only interpretation supplies that meaning to the dialogue writer and authored fallback. Common contracted/look-like wording is supported. Negative, private, hypothetical and reported wishes do not establish that personal wish. An explicit clarification can change what she understands. No transformation, contact, infection, purchase, consent or new point award follows from this metadata.
- **Literal idioms:** unexplained ducks/cake/raining-animals/broken-leg phrases retain literal referents or require a specific clarification. Local validation catches self-decoding, including the captured “talking is easy / super simple” paraphrase. Once the player explains the meaning, ordinary understanding is allowed. This is a bounded recognizer, not universal idiom coverage or a requirement to misunderstand direct compliments.
- **Source-backed assertions:** the writer receives compact evidence derived from the actual current room, completed controller result and canonical character dossier. Previous generated replies are explicitly not new object/biography authority. Validation checks mapped staged props, unrecorded family and former-life anecdotes, first-attempt/spouse learning stories and unrecorded cosmetic transfer. Plain third-person staging and asterisk staging are both covered. Supported facts, ordinary expressive gestures, reported player stories and hypotheticals have counterexamples in tests.
- **Safe ordinary-dialogue fallback:** if a writer/correction fails after a dialogue-only planner result, the displayed fallback is a character-specific clarification, rather than “Conversation only. No physical action was requested.” Tits’s fallback preserves the concrete wish or literal question. The planner/controllers are not rerun, and action/permission/payment receipts keep their real result.
- **Body packet:** remove the unconditional instruction to describe illness sensations. Read-only body observations omit unrelated hunger/bladder/fatigue state and retain explicitly recorded current symptoms. Current-only regions, reached milestones and clothing remain; old/future body snapshots do not enter the request.
- **Missing body detail:** distinguish a reached ankle change from a calf, knee or thigh description that is not recorded. Questions about an unrecorded specific subregion return the actual known detail and do not invoke AI to invent the rest. At stage 3, the calf question reports only the recorded ankle/balance change; at the reached calf milestone it is no longer classified as missing. Neither case claims a calf is unchanged or predicts its future shape.
- **Current-body response projection:** discard unsupported clinical/condition assurances and former-body comparisons as claim categories, while preserving supported anatomy. Current-only validation also rejects invented footwear/skin fusion and denial of reached leg changes. Empty or otherwise contradictory replies retain the existing single correction and authored fallback. This is scoped body-observation filtering, not general gesture removal, a word quota or a new model.
