# Charity household starting stock — proposal for review

## Existing decisions checked

Approved menu: toast/eggs or oatmeal/tea; sandwich/fruit; reheatable soup or chicken-vegetable stew and actual leftovers. Many local characters bring real meals. Monday groceries/toiletries come through Only on Time and are paid from personal funds, separate from shop money. Approved work/rest style includes ordinary neutral clothes, comfortable flats, practical sleepwear, mild care products. The player borrows her actual worn outfit; she uses a different clean spare after showering.

The approved morning sequence and private-room graph are retained. Existing player-clothing and household adapters track physical items, conditions, hampers, washing and collection; use those contracts for Charity’s own actor rather than inventing infinite replacement stock or moving the player when she does a chore. No household controller/start inventory is created by this draft.

## Replenishment and missing stock

Propose Monday **top-up to the approved target**, paying only for missing supplied items from her actual personal funds; do not add another full week to unlimited unsold stock every render. Her existing community visitors and actual leftovers remain independent sources. This top-up method, exact initial counts, prices/budget and recipe/use quantities require owner review before implementation.

No hidden withdrawals from the till/player, fabricated free credit, automatic delivery completion or guaranteed replacement for a missing courier is approved. Record shortages and use only genuine available alternatives; do not claim an unprepared meal or an unworn clean outfit exists. Quest coverage remains deferred and household stock implementation cannot silently activate it.
