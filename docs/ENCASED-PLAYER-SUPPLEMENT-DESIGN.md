This file is the required design checkpoint for general internally trapped/encased players. It is NOT an implemented escape/mastery route. Track ENC-01 in OPEN-TASKS-AND-DECISIONS.

## Settled constraints

- Mastered Kibbles never overrides the player's intentions and has will-based release. No zipper or seam exists, mastered or unmastered.
- Unmastered full encasement needs authored internal resistance/mastery/escape paths. External helpers and internal actions are distinct systems.
- Preserve the existing combat guide's anatomical restrictions, action budgets, resource ownership, original-profile preservation and entity-specific control rules. Do not grant mastery simply for elapsed time, chat count or a generic combat Resist Mental click.
- Hangry Fangs recognizes successful mastery, but its post-defeat internal route cannot be advertised as playable until this supplement and the Kibbles-specific continuation are defined and tested.

## Decisions required before implementation

1. Available actions from partial/full encasement, conscious/incapacitated states and after encounter defeat.
2. Attributes/DCs, costs, success accumulation, failure effects, retries, cooldowns and recovery; what changes when external threats remain.
3. External combatant versus captive action ownership, communication/intent, target selection and how resistance affects Main/Minor budgets. Keep accepted entity exceptions in the combat guide authoritative.
4. Difference between escaping, temporary control, permanent mastery and cooperative/relationship-based mastery; entity-specific acquisition and once-only outcomes.
5. Kibbles routines, habits, preferences, influence limits and relationship gains/damage that create alternate opportunities.
6. Helper intervention eligibility and authored reduction/escape power; no automatic peeling or rescue inferred from the help-seeking simulation.
7. Persistent state, reload at each stage, depleted resources, unavailable actions, quest reporting and regression tests.
