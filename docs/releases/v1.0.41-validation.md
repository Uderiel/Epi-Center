## v1.0.41 — approved Kibbles enemy abilities and AI candidate

Grapple earns head/neck/hand contact without free transformation; connected Skin Wrap continues on retained attachment after restraint ends. Bite is disabled at any head coverage. Legal Bite/Grapple or Bite/Wrap choices use the approved mix; head-occupied wrapping has priority. Prior unearned right-hand contact at encounter start is removed.

Wriggle Free uses the approved first-failure recovery / threshold Bite fallback, then hidden one/two retry cycles; occupied-head attached wrapping takes priority.
