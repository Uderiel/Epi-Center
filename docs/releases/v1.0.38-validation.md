## v1.0.38 — approved alignment ties and Confuse clarification
Owner approved: when alignment scores tie, compare Fitness (Physical), Reasoning (Mental), and Influence (Arousal) among the tied archetypes. Highest selects techniques automatically. If those also tie, player chooses only among the remaining tied archetypes, once per encounter; choice persists and spends no action/resource. This is a tie resolution, not an additional Hybrid archetype. Moves retain their individual Stamina/Resolve/split costs. Existing active saves can make the required choice without resetting spent allowances.

Confuse is a Mental Main move and is usable against Kibbles. A successful application affects the next attack: self-damage is 25% of attack damage; non-damaging or missed attacks yield zero and consume the effect. Recovery does not consume it. Engine preserves fractional damage exactly (10 → 2.5) instead of inventing a rounding rule. Kibbles still has Grapple/Skin Wrap in the fixture; Bite is not implemented in this bounded correction. Confuse 10 RES and Distract 5+5 remain provisional costs; this approval settles targeting/effect and alignment ties, not those numbers.

Validation: 34/34 deterministic adapter/engine cases plus entity assertions; browser renderer/adapter fixture verified exact-tie choices, Mental cost labels, serialization, Confuse availability/cost, remaining Minor, opponent review, zero self-damage narration, and Arousal split-cost labels. Existing three save keys and character records unchanged. Prior v1.0.37 browser results remain historical, not claimed as rerun. Production deployment/live verification recorded separately.
