# Priya completion package — cat route review draft

Status: owner-review proposal, not implementation or publication. Branch `rebuild/1.0-foundations`; public stays v1.0.104, runtime1931b6c. The daily-life rhythm in PRIYA-DAILY-LIFE-REVIEW-DRAFT.md is now approved in principle. New defaults below need review; preserved decisions are distinguished explicitly.

## Confirmed companion decisions

- The flats can recur during later valid weekend jogs, separately tracked per save/player. Track the same physical pair through park, wearer and closet; no duplicated shoes or cross-slot discovery state.
- Once the player has conquered the flats' binding/mastery system, revisiting does not reset earned mastery or demand another recovery grind. They may simply experience the footwear form. Being unworn and genuine solitude remain mandatory for reversion, including Priya/visitors absent from the apartment when applicable. A single successful recovery roll is not a new automatic mastery award.
- Offered/accepted snacks and drinks must become actual usable player inventory/Items-tab servings. Real preparation consumes stock once; Use consumes that exact serving once. Preserve additions and saved ownership, migrate old prepared servings without cloning, retain appropriate used containers/dishes, and support declining offers. AI text alone cannot mint a serving.
- Hold publication until this cat design is reviewed, the approved package is implemented, and regression checks pass. No runtime change is claimed by this draft.

## 1. How the arrangement begins

**Preserved:** respectful friendship/flirting can lead directly to a date. An excessively forward approach may prompt Priya to suggest a voluntary cat stay as a test of staying power. This is one possible reaction, not a punishment automatically triggered by keywords. Established friends retain ordinary visit/relationship routes independent of either curse.

**Proposed:** one explicit offer with clear accept/decline controls and a plain-language summary of expected stay, form choice and any proposed collar. A declined offer does not transform the player, grant items or equate refusal with hostility. Cat agreement and collar agreement are separate. Priya remains unaware of the collar's concealed effect; author-level mechanics belong in appropriate player/system receipts, not sudden NPC omniscience.

## 2. The form itself

**Preserved:** her offered form is a black-and-white cat; choose female Preeti or male Pri. A player-owned cat can qualify without being renamed, reassigned or having mastery reset. Ownership of Priya's own human form is never awarded.

**Proposed defaults:** a normal adult domestic cat, with fur, four paws, tail, feline senses and movement. No human hands or aloud human speech. The original player retains private thought; an unmastered feline consciousness communicates through sensory images, urges and emotion. Cat scent/hearing are useful without granting remote knowledge.

Provisional attributes, solely for review: Endurance3, Fitness4, Intuition6, Reflexes8, Willpower4, Reasoning3, Stability4, Presence5, Insight4, Influence3 (44points). Derive resource pools and alignment using the shared engine; no invented training bonus, universal100-pool override or free healing. Body/sex choice does not change the prototype statistics. Propose Common mechanics for the offered form to reuse the established recovery rules; “rare opportunity” is not an approved rarity tier. An owned cat keeps its existing rarity and profile.

First transformation gets a full saved three-stage passage: feline body/senses, paws/tail/posture, complete cat capabilities. Preserve the original player's identity, appearance, belongings and actual resources. Once mastered, use existing voluntary form equip/release conventions. Do not import the flats' stash trigger or add automatic blindness, speech or movement to the wrong form.

## 3. Mastery and stay credit

**Preserved:** with Priya's offered form, master it and return to the originally presented identity before she gives the updated stay expectation. Draw3,4or5days once and save it. For an owned cat, “almost a week” is the current incomplete contract.

**Proposed:** owned-cat branch =6complete in-world days. Offered branch =72/96/120actual cat-hours after accepting the post-mastery agreement; initial mastery time does not secretly fulfill the later stay. Save the chosen duration and exact start/progress.

Only actual time as her cat counts. Roaming outside counts; Priya need not be home. Taking a break into another body resets stay credit but retains the drawn duration, permanent mastery, ownership and ordinary relationship history. Reload, midnight and repeated dialogue do not reroll or duplicate credit. A player can end the arrangement without being falsely recorded as having completed it.

For the unmastered offered form, propose the existing Common loop:100initial Influence, thirty-minute internal action cadence,3Resolve Resist cost, Desperate Resistance and Regain Composure,100mastery/0Influence gate. Use shared opposed rolls and universal2d20 advantage/disadvantage; no parallel home-grown dice system. Calm/safe versus distressed contexts must come from actual state. Preserve earned mastery permanently. No automatic influence gain from ordinary stroking/being held is assumed.

## 4. Living with Priya

**Proposed:** fold cat care into her approved daily life. Morning/evening food offers, an accessible water bowl, litter area in a quiet bathroom corner, a soft resting place and a modest scratch/play area in the neutral apartment. Use real items/stock and the existing physical-needs system; do not create unsupported medical or hydration mechanics. Food, water, grooming, play and affection are opportunities, not forced interactions every few minutes.

Priya works, writes, hosts and goes out while the cat remains free to use accessible spaces. Naps/perching/exploring create varied recorded moments without long blank stretches. The cat may approach, follow, hide, play, protest or decline attention. Cat capabilities determine which food/items can be used; a feline cannot operate a kettle or human wardrobe.

Her cat remains the same player on one physical schedule, never a separate offscreen duplicate. Existing cats/dogs react only if present and individually authored. Maya/Lena or a new animal cannot be invented merely to fill a social scene.

## 5. Roaming and home access

**Preserved:** Priya occasionally leaves a window open for the arrangement; roaming does not break the stay.

**Proposed:** add an explicitly mapped safe window/fire-escape route from Unit2F to ground-level Applewood exterior. Treat this as new proposed apartment geometry, not an existing implemented feature. No two-floor jump or teleport. Opening state and all connecting edges must be real; closed/blocked access pauses the route honestly. The ordinary front door also works when actually opened/accompanied. A human visitor cannot automatically use a cat-only opening. Return uses the same physical path.

## 6. Collar and changing recognition

**Preserved:** friendly willingness can prompt a separate collar offer. The non-breakaway collar secretly increases Priya's pet-only belief by5percentage points per four eligible game-hours with the cat present with her. No gain while she is away. Collar removal immediately reverses its perception effect. The wearer cannot revert to human while wearing it; the collar does not fall off through reversion.

**Proposed:** start at0, cap100, retain partial intervals across saves/absence, and count actual shared room/tile presence. Use gradual recognition states rather than an abrupt personality rewrite: knows the agreement; increasingly slips into pet language; finds the prior identity harder to connect; at100primarily interprets the wearer as her pet.100is not death, permanent form loss or automatic failure. Ordinary affection can remain; do not invent cruelty or erase stored history.

A dedicated “protest collar” intent toggles persistent protest, with “settle/stop” pausing it. Scratching/rubbing/hind-paw action descriptions can request the same recorded intent; repeated clicks never grant more rolls. Preserve unfinished protest time and threshold reductions.

**Preserved removal mechanics:** one hidden1d50check each four eligible present-hours; initial requirement50. Every four eligible protest-hours reduces the requirement by1to a floor15. Apply earned reduction before that interval's check (proposed ordering). Absence pauses both clocks; quiet time pauses protest accrual only. The35reductions to reach the floor require140eligible protest-hours and still do not guarantee removal. Priya removes it out of concern/annoyance, without discovering its concealed function.

**Proposed after removal:** restore recognition of the agreed cat/person link immediately, retain actual memories/events without magically explaining the hidden curse, and require a new explicit collar agreement before re-equipping. Preserve previously earned protest/removal-threshold progress for this unique collar instead of resetting on reload/re-equip. A separate kitten name, kennel or “being treated as a pet” does not add a reversion lock without further approval. Outside the collar lock, use normal living-form reversion rules; do not automatically impose the flats' special solitude rule on cats.

## 7. Completion and a real date

**Preserved:** completion may lead Priya to offer a date, depending on the relationship. It does not automatically create romance, intimacy or a completed outing. She recognizes the original presented appearance/voice and the agreed cat link, not every secretly owned form.

**Proposed:** a grounded invitation to an implemented venue, with actual scheduling, travel, conversation and an ending. Offer a home tea/rom-com evening if a suitable public venue is unavailable. Keep acceptance, attendance, relationship change and physical boundaries as separate events. Ordinary friends can decline, postpone or prefer friendship without repeating the cat requirement.
